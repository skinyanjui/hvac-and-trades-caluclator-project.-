# Advertise on X

## Business Context
- **Type/Industry:** Advertising platform for businesses
- **What they do:** X Ads enables businesses to reach real-time conversations, drive conversions, and achieve measurable ROI.
- **Target audience:** Marketing teams, ecommerce brands, agencies, and growth-focused businesses across all sizes
- **Page goal:** Drive signups to X Ads Manager and expert consultations by demonstrating product value, ROAS proof, and activation workflows

# Page Layout & Structure

### Hero
Left-aligned h1 "Real-time conversations. Real results" with tight letter-spacing in near-black on white background. Subheading in gray: "Reach high-intent audiences, participate in real-time conversations, and drive measurable business results with X Ads." Right side features a 1.95:1 image (campaign cards flowing along branching pipeline) with overlaid badges: "10x ROAS average, never below 4x weekly" (linked anchor to testimonials) and "$500 Ad Credit" (linked anchor to offer). Primary CTA button "Get started" (near-black background, white text, rounded pill) anchors bottom-left; secondary link "Talk to an Ads expert" in gray sits adjacent. Above the fold also includes an internal link "Go to Testimonials" and anchor to performance section.

### Header / Navigation
Fixed vertical sidebar (logo at top, hamburger icon) with 20 navigation links grouped by category: Introduction, Basics, Advertising (expandable, exposes Overview, Get started, Best practices, Measurement, Success Stories), Products, Resources, Help Center, Blog, Premium Business. No primary CTA in header; header is informational-only for internal navigation.

### Primary CTA / Input
Two persistent buttons: "Get started" (near-black pill, white text) linking to X login redirect for Ads Manager; "Talk to an Ads expert" (white or outlined pill) linking to contact form anchor. Both sit in hero, repeated below major content sections.

### Why Advertise on X
- **Purpose:** Establish four core value drivers before feature/format sections.
- **Layout:** Subheading "Why advertise on X?" + intro copy "The internet talks here first". Followed by 4 article cards in a uniform grid (1 col each stacked vertically, or 2×2 if viewport wide); each card = h3 heading (Real-time conversations, Reach influential audiences, Own the moment, Earn attention, Precise targeting, Built for every business) + supporting paragraph. No images; white background cards with near-black text.

### Get $500 in Ad Credit
- **Purpose:** Limited-time incentive to reduce friction.
- **Layout:** Standalone section with h3 "Get $500 in ad credit", explanatory subtext "When you spend $500. New advertisers eligible. Terms apply.", and "Get Started" button (near-black pill, white text) linking to ad credit form.

### Built for Business Impact
- **Purpose:** Proof via ROAS metrics, case study testimonial, and CTA cluster.
- **Layout:** h3 "Built for business impact" + subheading "Backed by measurable results". Two primary CTAs: "Create your Ad" (near-black pill) and "Talk to an Ads expert" (outlined). Below: list of 4 metric cards (statistic + description pairs: 2.4x, 18%, 4.74x, 85%) in a bento grid (2 cols, 8 rows; the first cell is 1×1, second is 1×1, etc., with the final stat card spanning 2 cols). Then a dark-background (near-black) testimonial block with white text ("Despite the challenges… 10x ROAS…") + company logo (Skytech Gaming) at bottom. Carousel buttons below ("Show testimonial 1/2/3") to cycle through testimonials.

### Start Advertising on X (Carousel/Tabs Section)
- **Purpose:** Walk through 4-step campaign setup workflow.
- **Layout:** h3 "Start advertising on X" + subheading "Launch your campaign in minutes". Horizontal 4-step tab bar (numbered buttons: 01 Choose your objective, 02 Define your targeting, 03 Create your ad, 04 Launch and optimize) with underline indicator on active step. Right side navigation arrows (Previous/Next) to cycle steps. Main content area shows full-width 1.95:1 screenshot of the Ads Manager UI for the active step, with explanatory text overlay on the right. Step 1 copy: "Choose the outcome you want to achieve. Whether your goal is sales, leads, website traffic, engagement, video views, or app installs, we'll help optimize your campaign around the results that matter most to your business." CTAs ("Get started", "Talk to an Ads expert") remain visible below content.

### Find the Right Format (Numbered List / Carousel)
- **Purpose:** Showcase 7 ad format options with inline CTAs.
- **Layout:** h3 "Find the right format" + subheading "Built to drive results". Vertical list of 7 items (numbered 01–07), each item = format number badge + h3 name + description paragraph + "Create this format" link. Items: Amplify, Image + carousel ads, Collection + shoppable ads, Promoted posts, Dynamic product ads, Boost existing posts, Mentions Boost. Each entry aligns left, no grid; white background.

### Connect with an X Ads Specialist (Contact Form)
- **Purpose:** Sales-qualified lead capture.
- **Layout:** h3 "Connect with an X Ads specialist" + intro "Our team will reach out within one business day to help set up your first campaign, recommend strategy, or answer questions." Form with 6 text fields (First Name, Last Name, Company Name, Company Handle, Business Email Address, Phone Number) + 1 dropdown (Payment Method) + 1 dropdown (Country). Fields use light gray backgrounds with rounded corners and near-black placeholder text. Submit button (near-black pill, white text) sits below the form.

### Your Questions, Answered (FAQ / Accordion)
- **Purpose:** Reduce activation friction with common objections.
- **Layout:** h3 "Your questions, answered". List of 6 FAQ items as collapsible accordion sections (each heading clickable to reveal answer paragraph). Questions: Is there a minimum budget to advertise? How do I get started? How quickly can my campaign go live? What kind of businesses can advertise? Do I need marketing experience? Can I get help setting up my first campaign? What advertising formats are available?

### Footer
- **Purpose:** Secondary navigation, branding, compliance.
- **Layout:** 3-column footer (Platform, Corp, Help; Business resources, Developer resources; Product nav links). Contains X logo, heading hierarchy (h2 for column heads), near-black text on white background, rounded corners on link elements.

**Notable patterns:** 
- Primary color: near-black (text, CTAs, accents); secondary surface: white; testimonial & metric cards use light gray backgrounds (50% opacity black).
- Metric cards in bento grid (2 cols, mixed row spans); final stat spans full width.
- Buttons: two states — near-black pill (primary) and outlined white/gray (secondary); all rounded.
- Image ratios consistent at 1.95:1 for hero and step carousel screenshots.
- Two type families; heading letter-spacing tight (negative kerning on display h1).
- Navigation fixed left; no sticky header bar.
- All form inputs: light gray background, rounded corners, focus-visible indicator honored, prefers-reduced-motion respected.
